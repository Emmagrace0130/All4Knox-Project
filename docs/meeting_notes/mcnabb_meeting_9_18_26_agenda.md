## 1. TennCare dose limits: the Clinical Summary vs the May 2026 BESMART update

**What the app does now:** follows the BESMART update, and shows the Clinical
Summary figure beside it with a note that they differ.

| Prescriber | Clinical Summary 2026 (slide 2) | BESMART Provider Education, May 28 2026 (slides 4–5) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BESMART MD/DO | 20 mg, or 24 mg with addiction consultation; no PA | **32 mg/day** for preferred bup/nal products, no PA; PA for non-preferred products, including monoproduct |
| Non-BESMART MD/DO | PA required; limit 16 mg | **≤ 16 mg without PA**; PA for > 16 mg up to 32 mg; without an approved PA above 16 mg, taper to 8 mg after 6 months |
| NP/PA | 16 mg | 16 mg ("no changes for mid-level prescribers") |

**Ask:**
- Is following the May 2026 update right for TennCare patients?
- Will slide 2 be revised, and does McNabb practice already use 32 mg?
- Slide 11 says "an arbitrary limit of 20 mg for most physicians, unless they
  are addiction specialists." Is that describing state law (item 3), and
  should it stay as written?

## 2. Can a non-BESMART NP/PA prescribe for a TennCare patient?

The Clinical Summary (slide 2) has a non-BESMART pathway for NPs and PAs: PA
required, 16 mg. The BESMART update (slide 5 footnote) says: *"All mid-level
prescribers must be BESMART."*

**What the app does now:** keeps the slide 2 pathway and marks it as an open
question.

**Ask:** does that pathway exist, or should an NP/PA without BESMART be told
they cannot prescribe for TennCare patients?

## 3. State documentation and consultation thresholds alongside the 32 mg payer limit

A 32 mg payer limit doesn't remove the state thresholds. TN guidelines, Section
II.D (p. 19), which match TCA § 53-11-311 (Appendix H, pp. 43–46):

- above **16 mg/day for more than 30 consecutive days**: document why the higher
  dose is required;
- above **20 mg/day for more than 30 consecutive days**: consult with or refer
  to an addiction specialist.

**What the app does now:** shows both as considerations on the changed TennCare
pathways.

**Ask:** is that the right framing for a prescriber at McNabb?

## 4. First-dose COWS threshold: 7 or 11?

The TN guidelines give two numbers:

- Appendix C (p. 30): *"Give first dose when COWS score ≥ 7."*
- Section II.D (p. 17): for **office-based** induction, *"a COWS score of 11 or
  higher is recommended."*

The Clinical Summary's induction content gives no COWS number.

**What the app does now:** the assistant shows both, with their sources. The
induction tool doesn't use COWS.

**Ask:**
- Which should the toolkit present?
- Should the planned COWS calculator (project plan 3.1) exist, and if so, with
  which threshold?

## For the demo: known limitation to say out loud

Sections stop the assistant attributing one source's rule to another. They do
not stop it misreading a passage within a single source. In testing, one run
applied the statute's 16 mg NP/PA limit to physicians. Every answer lists the
passages it used, and those are the check.
