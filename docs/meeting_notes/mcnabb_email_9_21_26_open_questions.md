**To:** Dr. Ryan Alexander
**Cc:** Emma
**Subject:** All4Knox — questions before we add the new BESMART and MAT checklist material

Dr. Alexander,

We've added the BESMART guide, the MAT care checklists, the clinic readiness
checklists and the phased roadmap to the All4Knox project, and we're planning
how to use them. Before we change what the toolkit tells prescribers, a few
points need your decision. Most can be answered in a line, and we're happy to
go through them on a short call instead.

**Where our sources disagree**

1. **Non-BESMART NPs and PAs with TennCare patients.** The BESMART guide says
   NPs and PAs who are not BESMART-enrolled cannot prescribe buprenorphine to
   TennCare patients. TennCare's May 28, 2026 provider education says "all
   mid-level prescribers must be BESMART." The toolkit currently follows slide 2
   of the Clinical Summary: prior authorization required, 16 mg limit. Should
   we change it to say they cannot prescribe for TennCare patients, and point
   them to BESMART enrollment or referral?

2. **TennCare dose limits.** TennCare's May 2026 update lets BESMART MD/DOs
   prescribe up to 32 mg a day of preferred products without prior
   authorization, and lets non-BESMART MD/DOs prescribe up to 16 mg without it.
   The toolkit follows that update and shows the Clinical Summary's 20 mg
   (24 mg with an addiction consult) beside it. The BESMART guide, dated March,
   still refers to prescribing "below 16/20mg" to avoid the PA form. Is the May
   update what you want the toolkit to use? If so, that line in the guide may
   need updating before it's shared further. Alongside the payer limit, the
   toolkit also shows the state guideline's thresholds: above 16 mg a day for
   more than 30 days, document why; above 20 mg a day for more than 30 days,
   consult or refer to an addiction specialist. Is that the right way to
   present them?

3. **COWS before the first dose.** The TN guidelines give a score of 7 or more
   in Appendix C, and 11 or more for office-based induction in Section II.D.
   Which should the toolkit use?

4. **Oxycodone wait time.** Slide 4 says to wait 12 hours; slide 5 branches at
   more than 24 hours since the last dose. Which is right? The toolkit shows
   both for now.

**About the new documents**

5. **Authorship.** Who prepared these, are they final, and may we credit them
   to the McNabb Center? We want to cite them correctly. Two of the files (the
   full care checklist and the Tennessee readiness checklist) have no named
   author. One roadmap slide mentions standardizing "if CMG-wide", which reads
   as though it was written for another organization.

6. **UDS and CSMD at every visit.** Both care checklists list a urine drug
   screen and a CSMD check as standing items at every visit. The state
   guidelines don't set one general frequency. Should the toolkit present
   every-visit testing as McNabb's protocol, or as one reasonable option?

7. **Adding checklist items to the tools.** We'd like to add care-checklist
   items to tool results where they apply:
   - in the induction tool: consent, baseline UDS, CSMD and labs, and naloxone;
   - in the UDS tool: non-punitive steps after unexpected results;
   - in the dosing tool: refills, a single pharmacy, and pill counts.

   We won't add questions unless an answer changes the recommendation. May we
   send you a one-page list to mark up?

8. **Clinic readiness material.** The readiness checklists and roadmap are
   about setting up a MAT program rather than treating a patient. We plan a
   separate guided "clinic readiness" walkthrough for them, clearly labeled as
   operational guidance. Should you review that content, or someone on the
   operations side?

**Still open**

9. **Clinical sign-off.** Will you review the 30 content blocks yourself, or
   name someone? We'll set up their account and walk them through the review
   page.

10. **Documents and contacts.** If it's easier, point us to the right person
    for any of these:
    - the current BESMART Program Description (ours is from March 2023);
    - whether the TennCare prior authorization criteria memo and the Optum Rx
      PA form linked in the guide are current;
    - address, phone and payer details for McNabb's own referral listing;
    - who at McNabb handles approval of externally hosted apps.

Until we hear back, the toolkit stays as it is, and every result continues to
state that it hasn't yet been clinically reviewed.

Thank you,
Gerald Jones
All4Knox team (with Emma) · Applied Systems Lab, University of Tennessee, Knoxville
