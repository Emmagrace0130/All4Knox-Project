**Recommended, not yet agreed: do not ingest the BESMART guide or the care
checklists as assistant reference collections.** Approved items should enter as
toolkit content instead. The reasons:

- Toolkit content is reviewed block by block, and a review is bound to the
  exact text reviewed. Reference collections are searched as-is and have no
  per-passage sign-off. That is acceptable for a published state guideline, but
  not for documents whose authorship is unconfirmed.
- The BESMART guide carries a superseded threshold (see Q2). Ingesting it would
  give the BESMART answer section two contradictory dose figures.
- The primary TennCare documents are already in the corpus, and they cover the
  same payer rules at their source.

Once toolkit content is approved, it reaches the assistant automatically
through the toolkit collection.

## 7. Guardrails

- Readiness content never enters the four clinical rule engines, the clinical
  content blocks, or the clinical and payer answer sections.
- Nothing from these files is marked reviewed on the strength of its author.
  A review exists only when someone records it in `/review`.
- Every clinical text change goes through `frontend/src/content/` and the §3a
  regenerate-and-test pipeline. Never hand-edit the backend JSON.
- Ingest one canonical format per document, and label every ingested passage
  with its date. A figure that later guidance supersedes is labelled as
  superseded, never silently kept.
